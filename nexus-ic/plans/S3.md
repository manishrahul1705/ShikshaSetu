## 3. S2 Lifecycle Transitions & `case_actions` Plugin Fields

### S2 Lifecycle Transitions Used:
- **Transfer Initiation**: Calls `transitionStatus(caseDoc._id, 'Transfer Requested', user, reason)` in `server/src/modules/lifecycle/lifecycleService.js`.
  - Sets `caseDoc.transferStatus = 'REQUESTED'`.
  - Case status moves to `'Transfer Requested'`.
  - `currentOwner` retains the original sender officer/bureau as the accountable owner.
- **Transfer Acceptance**: Calls `transitionStatus(caseDoc._id, 'Transfer Accepted', user, 'Transfer accepted by receiving bureau')`.
  - Sets `caseDoc.transferStatus = 'ACCEPTED'`.
  - Case status moves to `'Transfer Accepted'` (and subsequently `'Under Process'`).
  - Switched owner ("New Owner Active"):
    - Updates `caseDoc.lastAccountableOwner = { office: prevOwner.office, role: prevOwner.role, officerId: prevOwner.officerId, officerName: prevOwner.officerName, at: new Date() }`.
    - Updates `caseDoc.currentOwner = { office: toBureau, role: 'bureau_officer', officerId: acceptingUser._id, officerName: acceptingUser.displayName, assignedAt: new Date() }`.
    - Updates `caseDoc.owningBureau = toBureau`.
- **Transfer Rejection**:
  - Sets `caseDoc.transferStatus = 'REJECTED'`.
  - Reverts status from `'Transfer Requested'` back to `'Under Process'`.
  - Case returns to sender's active queue with rejected reason; `currentOwner` remains unchanged.
- **Invariant Guard (`lifecycleGuards.js`)**:
  - `validateStatusTransition` guarantees that once a case has `transferStatus !== 'NONE'` or non-empty `transferHistory`, it can never transition to `'Registered'` or `'Submitted'`.

### `case_actions` Plugin Fields (Registered in `server/src/models/plugins.js`):
In `server/src/modules/actions/plugins/caseAction.plugin.js`:

## 6. Open Questions & Architectural Clarifications

1. **Who receives escalations for overdue unacknowledged transfers?**
   - *Design Choice*: The supervisors of **both** the sending and receiving bureaus receive the alert notification (`DEMO-ESCALATION-v1`), and the case status remains with the sending bureau's accountable owner without moving the case.
2. **Holiday calendar for business days?**
   - *Design Choice*: As specified in `RULES.md` and prompt, Saturday and Sunday are excluded. No holiday calendar is invented. This is explicitly noted in `FEATURES.md` and `README.md`.
3. **Who can access the shared institutional view (`/sync/:institutionPid`)?**
   - *Design Choice*: Authorized officer roles (`bureau_officer`, `supervisor`, `regional_officer`, `competent_authority`, `system_admin`). Cases belonging to other bureaus or restricted regions that the officer cannot access are aggregated and displayed strictly as `"N restricted entries"` without leaking case titles, narratives, or IDs.
4. **Communication Log Content**:
   - *Design Choice*: Conforms to SPEC 15.1: `communication_log` stores only `{ caseId or coordinationId, from, to, channel, at }`. Zero complaint text, message body, or reporter identity is ever stored in the communication log.
5. **Contract for S5 Closure Gate**:
   - *Design Choice*: S3 exports helper functions `hasOpenTransfers(caseId)` and `hasPendingTasks(caseId)` for S5's PROOF closure gate to verify that all transfers and tasks are completed before closure is permitted.
